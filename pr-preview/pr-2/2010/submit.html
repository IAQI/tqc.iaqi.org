Notification and submission of extended abstract

All submitted manuscripts were reviewed by the Program Committee, and notification of acceptance or rejection were made on 11th February 2010. Please note that presenters not registered by 1st March 2010 will have their submission automatically withdrawn. On-line registration is available at the TQC 2010 web site since early February 2010.

Authors of accepted papers for oral presentations or a poster session should then prepare a short abstract (at most two pages on A4- or Letter-size paper using at least 11-point fonts) to be included in the workshop abstract book. After the acceptance, please submit the abstract by 1st March 2010. The format is the same as the format for poster submission. Abstracts should be sent in PDF format by email to tqc2010leeds.ac.uk
