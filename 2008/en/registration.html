Registration

Registration Fees

The registration fee includes the workshop abstract book and a banquet ticket.

  • Early Registration (until Dec 20 inclusive)
  • 15,000 yen (Regular)
  • 5,000 yen (Student)

  • Late Registration (from Dec 21, 2007 to Jan 23, 2008)
  • 20,000 yen (Regular)
  • 8,000 yen (Student)

  • Additional Banquet Ticket
  • 5,000 yen

Registration Procedure

  1. Register at
    https://www.brl.ntt.co.jp/cgi-bin/cs/tqc2008_registration/index.php
    You will receive an email including your confirmation number.

  2. Pay the registration fee using PayPal links below. Follow the instructions you see when you click on the PayPal link. You need to enter your comfirmation number in the PayPal form. Even if you do not have a PayPal account, payment by credit card is possible through the PayPal link.

    Regular (Late Registration)
    Regular: 20,000 yen
    Regular with one additional banquet ticket: 25,000 yen
    Regular with two additional banquet tickets: 30,000 yen
    Regular with three additional banquet tickets: 35,000 yen

    Student (Late Registration)
    Student: 8,000 yen
    Student with one additional banquet ticket: 13,000 yen
    Student with two additional banquet tickets: 18,000 yen
    Student with three additional banquet tickets: 23,000 yen
