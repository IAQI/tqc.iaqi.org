Registration

Register Here

There are early-bird, regular, and virtual-only registration options. Early-bird registration ends May 31, 2022. The last day to register is June 26, 2022.

******* Early Bird Registration before June 1st
Standard400 USD
Student300 USD
******* Regular Registration June 1st and after
Standard500 USD
Student400 USD
******* Virtual Only
Standard75 USD
Student25 USD

Included in the registration fee is admission to the poster session, a conference dinner, and lunch buffets during the conference. Virtual attendance just allows for online streaming of the talks.

In the event that a paper needs to be presented virtually, the standard in-person registration fee will be assessed.

Student Support
There is funding available to support students in attending TQC 2022. To apply for student support, please send an email to tqc.2022.conference@gmail.com with the subject “Application for student support”, containing the following information:

  • Name & home institution
  • Title of talk or poster presentation (if applicable)
  • How much financial support you are receiving through your advisor or alternative funds
  • If applicable, a statement declaring membership in an underrepresented group

The recipients of student support will be selected at the discretion of the TQC local organizing committee. For full consideration, please submit by June 1, 2022.
