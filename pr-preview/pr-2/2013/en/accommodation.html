Information for Foreign Nationals Regarding Visas

Some countries and territories must apply for a travel visa before traveling to Canada. To see if your country is on this list, please click here. If you are traveling from one of the countries on this list it is strongly advised that you begin your visa application process as soon as possible.

Please email to tqc2013@gmail.com to contact the Conference Coordinator for more information and to request an invitation letter. For security reasons invitation letters can only be issued to authors or registered attendees of the conference.

Please be sure to include all of the following information in your original email: 1. Your name as it appears on your passport; 2. Your current postal mailing address; 3. If you have any papers accepted for the conference, please provide the title.

All these information are necessary, and omitting any of them will delay the issuing of the support letter. You will receive a PDF letter by email within 4-5 business days.

Travel


By Air

Those travelling by air may fly into Pearson International Airport, Hamilton International Airport, Kitchener-Waterloo International Airport, Toronto City Airport or Buffalo Niagara International Airport. Red Car Service provides airport transportation to and from each of these airports.

Pearson International Airport (YYZ) 

  • 70 km (50 minutes) to Guelph
  • car rental

Hamilton International Airport (YHM)

  • 65 km (55 minutes) to Guelph
  • car rental 

Kitchener-Waterloo International Airport (YKF): 

  • 21 km (25 minutes) to Guelph
  • car rental    

Toronto City Airport (YTZ): 

  • 90 km (75 minutes) to Guelph
  • Car rental service is not directly available at Toronto City Airport. To rent a vehicle, please contact your favourite downtown rental service.
  • ground transportation  

Buffalo Niagara International Airport (BUF):    

  • 104 miles (2.5 hours) to Guelph.  You must cross the Canada/US border.
  • car rental  

Airport Tansportation: Red Car Service provides airport transportation to Guelph from all of these airports. 


By Rail

Via Rail offers two or three times daily service in each direction, between Toronto and London, with connections to points east and west.    

Go Transit now offers both train and bus service to Guelph. Schedules are available here - select the Kitchener (formerly Georgetown) corridor.


By Bus

Greyhound and Aboutown Northlink (to and from Hamilton) offer service to Guelph.
