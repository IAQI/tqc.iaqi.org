Registration

The on-line registration and application for funding for the 8th Conference on Theory of Quantum Computation,Communication and Cryptography, May 21-23, 2013 is now open on the Fields site at: http://www.fields.utoronto.ca/cgi-bin/register?form_selection=TQC2013.

The registration fee includes the workshop abstract book and a banquet ticket.

  • Early Registration (before April 30), $150 (Regular), $50 (GradStudent & PDF).
  • Late Registration (after April 30), $200 (Regular), $80 (GradStudent & PDF).
  • Undergraduate students are free. In this case, please present the student ID at the reception (the banquet ticket is not included but can be purchased when registering).
  • Additional Banquet Ticket $40.

Registration will be on-line to May 23. However attendees who register after May 7 will no longer receive a package due to the time cost of package preparation.
