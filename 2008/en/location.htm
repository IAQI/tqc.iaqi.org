Location

The workshop will be held at Koshiba Hall (Faculty of Science Bldg. 1)
in Hongo Campus, the University of Tokyo.
[Access Map/Campus Map]

From Narita Airport to Hongo Campus

The easiest way is to take a Keisei SKYLINER train to Keisei Ueno Station and take a taxi at the station.

Keisei Ueno Station is located in about 2km east from Hongo Campus. Thus, you may be able to walk from the station to the campus. Please see the following map.
