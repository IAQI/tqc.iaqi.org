General Information

Quantum computation, quantum communication, and quantum cryptography are subfields of quantum information processing that is a new interdisciplinary field of information science and quantum mechanics. TQC 2006 focuses on theoretical aspects of the subfields. The objective of the workshop is to bring together researchers in the subfields in order to interact with each other and to share problems and recent discoveries. The workshop will be held from February 22 to February 23, 2006. It will consist of invited and contributed talks.

Date: February 22-23, 2006
Place: NTT R&D Center, Atsugi, Kanagawa, Japan [access]
