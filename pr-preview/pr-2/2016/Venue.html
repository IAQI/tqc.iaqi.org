    Venue

    The venue will be the beautiful Baroque Magnus House in Berlin's city centre.



    Notice: Unfortunately the conference venue is not barrier-free. If this might affect you, please contact the local organizers at tqc2016@gmail.com.

    Travel 

    The Magnus House is close to the station Friedrichstraße (∼ 500 meters) which is a major hub in Berlin's excellent public transport network. 

    • Via Berlin main station: One stop with S-Bahn lines S5, S7 or S75. You can purchase a short-distance ticket (Kurzstrecke) for 1,70 € (∼ 10 minutes).
    • Via Tegel airport (TXL): Take the bus TXL to Berlin main station and change to S-Bahn lines S5, S7 or S75 (∼ 40 minutes). Ticket: Berlin AB - 2,70 €.
    • Via Schönefeld airport (SXF): The RB14 (direction Nauen) is the most convenient way to go directly to Friedrichstraße. Departing every 60 minutes at 7:03, 8:03, etc. the journey takes about 50 minutes. Alternatively, take the S9 to Ostkreuz and change to the S5, S7 or S75 to go to Friedrichstraße. This connection operates in 20min intervals and takes about 60 minutes. Ticket: Berlin ABC - 3,30 €.
