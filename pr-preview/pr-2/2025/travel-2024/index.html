Applying for a travel visa for Japan

Before booking your flights, please check whether you need a visa or not here.

The earliest you can apply for a visa is three months in advance of your arrival. You should receive an answer within seven days (but we still encourage you to apply not too close to your arrival date). Please check the relevant document list for the application with the Japanese embassy in your country of residence.

  • If you need to fill out the “Invitation letter” form for your application, feel free to use [this document], where we pre-filled some fields for you. Please add information in Relationship with visa applicant(s) field by listing your position and relation to the topic of quantum information/computing.
  • You should also additionally attach the confirmation of registration and the invitation letter which you will get in your email after completing the registration.
  • For the application, you will also need to attach “Itinerary in Japan” (also called “Schedule of stay” or “Itinerary during stay in Japan” or “Travel Itinerary”), which we (partially) pre-filled for you [Itinerary in Japan].
  • Finally, you may download a [brochure of the conference here] and attach it to your application.

If you need any additional documents from our side to support your application, please don’t hesitate to contact us at tqc@squids.ch.

Travelling to the conference

Reaching Okinawa

There are direct flights to Naha airport in Okinawa (OKA) from Taipei, Seoul, and Hong Kong as well as all the major Japanese airports (NRT, HND, and KIX). These give European and Asian travelers a relatively large number of entry points into Okinawa. Those coming across the Pacific are a little less likely to use anything other than NRT, HND, or maybe KIX.

There are ferry services between Okinawa and other regional destinations.

From the airport to OIST

From the airport, a shuttle bus (1600 JPY) or a taxi (12000 JPY) reaches OIST and Onna Village in 1h. Follow the directions to the Conference Center in the OIST Main Campus.

Shuttle Bus Service

Free shuttle buses will operate between OIST and main hotels located in Onna Village  (where OIST is situated) and Ishikawa. Buses will run according to the two routes below, stopping at bus stops nearby the hotels, though not directly in front of them. Depending on the location of a hotel and a bus stop, it may be necessary to walk approximately 5 to 20 minutes. Please refer to the map above for the bus route and nearby hotel information.

North Route (yellow on the map)

  1. Ishikawa Interchange
  2. Onna no eki  (Nakayukui Market mae)
  3. Moon Beach mae
  4. Tiger Beach mae
  5. Sunmarina Beach mae
  6. OIST Conference Center

South Route (blue on the map)

  1. Kariyushi Beach mae
  2. Halekulani Okinawa mae
  3. Hotel Miyuki Beach mae
  4. Seragaki Beach mae
  5. Nabee Beach mae
  6. Seaside House mae
  7. OIST Conference Center

Notes:

1. The above routes are not yet finalized and might be slightly changed.

2. There will be no free shuttle bus service available between Okinawa Naha Airport and OIST. Please refer to the information on public transport options.

Conference banquet venue

The venue for the conference banquet (Wednesday, September 11, from 7:00 PM) is Rizzan Sea-Park Hotel Tancha-Bay. The hotel is located at 〒904-0496 1496 Tancha, Onna Village, Kunigami, Okinawa. For detailed access information, please visit the hotel’s website: Rizzan Sea-Park Hotel Tancha-Bay Access Information.

Hotels

OIST is located in Onna, a resort town with a varied accommodation offering.  Some hotel recommendations are listed below with information on conference discounts (when available) and other available discounts. A small number of rooms in student accommodation will be distributed to participants in need.

Note that these are not necessarily the cheapest offers near the conference venue, just the ones that have agreements with OIST. We encourage you to widen your search if you have limited travel support from your institution. Also, considering September remains a busy tourist season in Okinawa, early booking can guarantee better availability of hotels and secure more favorable rates, so we strongly advise booking your hotel as early as possible. There are more hotels on the map than listed below.

Due to OIST’s location being a bit far from the town center, there is only one and rather small hotel within a short walking distance. To accommodate participants of TQC2024, a free shuttle bus service between nearby hotels will be provided. Please refer to the information on the free shuttle bus service for the bus route details.

Meals

Lunch will be provided in campus. Two evening events will provide food: the Industry Gala on Monday and the conference dinner on Wednesday.

Information on cafés, restaurants and shops in campus here.

Resources for participants with dietary restrictions:

  • Okinawa’s Vegetarian Guide Book. Includes a map of veggie-friendly venues and a guide you can take to restaurants to indicate dietary restrictions (e.g. plant-based, gluten-free, common allergies).
  • Is It Vegan Japan. Includes a good guide for shopping.
  • There will be an option at registration to note any dietary restrictions, and you will be catered to.

Student support

Applications for student support are now closed (notification 25th of May). TQC organizers will be able to offer:

  • Waiving of the conference fee for up to 30 students.
  • Accommodation in shared rooms for up to 26 students at the OIST Seaside House.
  • Additional travel support: TBA in May.

Priority will be given to students presenting a talk at the conference. The needs and means of individual institutions will be taken in consideration.

Poster printing

Okinawa Island offers limited options for printing posters, particularly of A0 size. One recommended print shop is ACCEA, situated in Naha City, which is approximately a one-hour drive from OIST. Due to the distance and limited availability, for your convenience we strongly advise printing your poster before departure and taking it with you.

ACCEA

  • Directions
  • Cost of A0 poster: around 5000JPY
  • Printing time: no less than 2 hours, in some cases ready by the next business day.
  • Opening hours: Weekdays 8:00-22:00, Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays 10:00-19:00
  • You can also print from web (translated website / original website in Japanese). Select Naha store (那覇店), the second-to-last option, as pick-up location.
