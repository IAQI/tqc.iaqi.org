Travel

Champaign-Urbana is conveniently located at the crossroads of Interstates I-57, I-74, and I-72, about a 2-3 hour drive from Chicago, Indianapolis, St. Louis, and Peoria.

The nearest airport is Willard Airport (CMI), which is serviced by American Airlines and offers convenient, non-stop service to Chicago O’Hare (ORD) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW). Connections are available from these major hubs to virtually anywhere on the globe. Willard Airport is located about 8 miles from the conference venue and hotels on the University of Illinois campus. Ground transportation is available by taxi or Uber/Lyft.

Chicago is about 150 miles to the north. You can fly into Chicago O’Hare (ORD) or Chicago Midway (MDW). Then you can either rent a car and drive the ~2.5 hours to Urbana, or you can travel by bus (Peoria Charter).

Located in the heart of downtown Champaign, Illinois Terminal offers visitors a magnificent facility through which one can access local city bus services, Amtrak trains, Greyhound and taxis.

Peoria Charter Coach Company also offers daily bus trips between the Chicago area and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.
